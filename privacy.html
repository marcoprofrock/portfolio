Privacy Policy

This website is a private portfolio used solely for job applications by Marco Pröfrock. It is not operated commercially. The processing of personal data is kept to the absolute minimum required to deliver the page.

Hosting & Server Logs

The site is hosted by a third-party provider (GitHub Pages). For technical reasons, the hosting provider temporarily processes connection data such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp in standard server log files. This data is necessary to deliver the website and ensure its security. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

Fonts

Fonts are loaded locally from this server. No data is transferred to external font providers (e.g. Google Fonts).

Chat Widget (Formspree)

The chat widget at the bottom of the page allows you to send a short message. If you choose to send a message, the content of your message and a timestamp are transmitted via the service Formspree (Formspree Inc., USA) to my email inbox. Use of the chat is entirely voluntary. Please do not enter any sensitive personal data. No additional metadata, IP geolocation, or device fingerprinting is collected. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through voluntary submission).

YouTube Videos

Embedded videos are loaded only after you actively click the play button, and only via the privacy-enhanced domain youtube-nocookie.com. No connection to YouTube is established before you click.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact hello@marco.ad.
