i love stuttgart

stadtwerke stuttgart · integrated campaign · 2026

problem

the energy transition is complex and mostly told as must and should. hard to love – especially for gen z and millennials. stadtwerke stuttgart wanted the city to fall for it anyway.

idea

treat it like a relationship: „aus liebe zu stuttgart" – out of love for stuttgart. and every relationship needs a coach. meet dr. kessel, named after the basin stuttgart sits in and played by merlin sandmeyer. he spots the red flags and teaches the city new love languages.

solution

four colours, one love coach. films and key visuals like „erkenn die red flags: zu viel kohle." ran on billboards, trams, the cannstatter wasen and social media – while i love stuttgart stickers turned up all over town.
